Academic Research

Academic research is anchored by two thesis projects. My MSc thesis, The Effects of Map Features in RTS Gameplay, investigates how measurable RTS map features such as path structure, choke points, base exits, symmetry, and resource safety can bias matches toward different MicroRTS agent strategies through evolved map datasets and quantitative analysis. My BSc thesis, Tracing Historical Paths, designed an intelligent AR tour guide that combined preference-aware pathfinding, a recommender system, and augmented-reality navigation cues to create more personalized heritage journeys instead of always defaulting to the shortest route.
